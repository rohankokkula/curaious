# Deck Template · Curaious Talk

Use this as a starting outline. Aim for 10–15 slides, 16:9, one idea per slide.

1. **Title**: talk title, your name, date.
2. **Agenda**: 3–4 bullets on what you'll cover.
3. **The problem**: what you're solving and why it matters.
4. **Context / background**: just enough for everyone to follow.
5–8. **The core**: your approach, method, or build. Use screenshots, diagrams, or a short demo over walls of text.
9. **What went wrong / what you'd do differently**: the honest part. This is usually the most useful slide for the room.
10. **Key takeaways**: 3 bullets max. What should people remember or try themselves?
11. **Resources / links**: anything you referenced or want to share.
12. **Thank you / Q&A**: your contact info if you're open to follow-ups.

Tips:
- Keep text large enough to read from across a room. Fewer words, bigger font.
- No confidential information: employer data, unpublished numbers, or anything you wouldn't want screenshotted.
- Export as a single PDF before uploading.
